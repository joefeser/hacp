completed standard, certified compliance framework, or proven vendor-neutral
implementation.

Use this navigation pack to pick the best starting point for your role, then
move to protocol detail as needed.

## Authority Boundary Reminder

HACP is not an agent runtime.
HACP is not a transport protocol.
HACP does not dispatch work, run tools, call models, write GitHub, approve
outcomes, accept risk, merge, deploy, or ship.
Validation output is evidence for review, not approval.
